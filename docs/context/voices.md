# What people say about the hand-off today

Notes from short conversations with the people who live with the Closed Won
hand-off. They're fictional, like everything in this case, and they don't
agree with each other.

**Maja Ek, Billing Specialist**

> I want to see every invoice before it goes to a customer. Every single one.
> When something's wrong, I'm the one who answers for it.

> Half my week is chasing POs and VAT numbers in Slack. Reps should sort that
> out before they close, not after.

**Kim Andersson, RevOps Manager**

> If someone still has to click 50 button for every deal, we've moved the
> copying, not removed it.

> Whatever we build, I need to trust it. And I don't want to be the person who
> relays every problem to whoever can fix it.

**Priya Shah, Account Executive**

> Please don't give me another form to fill in before I can close. I'll close
> it and fix what's missing after.

> I close a deal and it disappears. I find out something was wrong when the
> customer emails me three weeks later.

**Hannah Okafor, Relationship Manager**

> Customers hate being chased for a PO after they've signed. For a customer
> we've had for years, I'd rather we send the invoice and sort the PO out
> later.

**Lea Fischer, Customer Success Manager**

> The customer should have their workspace on the start date, invoice or no
> invoice. My kickoff call is booked for that day.

**Tom Becker, Credit Controller**

> Nobody gets Enterprise before we know we can bill them. And when a card
> payment fails, nobody tells me until the customer loses access and calls
> their rep.

**Alex Berg, Account Executive**

> I get pinged about everything already. One message a day with what needs me
> would be plenty.

**Johan Berg, IT Manager at Nordlys Energi (a customer)**

> Another vendor charged our card for a renewal after we'd signed an invoice
> deal with them. It took two months to get the money back.
